## Player Controls
Use the mouse to drag three ingredients into the cauldron. Mix them together and drag a potion bottle from the top shelf into the cauldron. Once your potion bottle is full, drop it on top of the customer to see what wacky potion you've made this time...

There are currently three endings, so good luck and have fun!


## Debugging Controls
To activate the debug menu, press "t". This will allow you to instantly create specific potions or automatically skip dialogue (Among some other information).
